# Filament guide and spool clip

Quick prints to stop the slack loops between the spool and the extruder.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `filament_guide.stl` | 12 mm tab with a slotted M3 hole, 40 mm arm, 12 mm eye with a 2.8 mm bore and an 8 mm funnel on top. |
| `spool_clip.stl` | U-clip for a spool rim 2–3 mm thick (2.6 mm gap), with a notch that holds the filament end. |

**Guide:** mount it under one of the extruder's M3 screws (use a screw 3 mm longer), so the eye sits in line with the extruder inlet, toward the spool. Thread the filament through the eye before loading. Print flat, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, no supports (the funnel is a cone opening upward).

**Clip:** push it onto the spool rim and tuck the filament end into the notch whenever you unload.

Also: wind the slack back onto the spool, align the spool with the extruder entrance, and add a little drag (a washer) so the spool doesn't overspin.
